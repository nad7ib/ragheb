Frequently Asked Questions

The ARH approval process involves preparing a comprehensive technical dossier, submitting it through the proper channels, and undergoing technical inspections. Our team manages all aspects of this process, from initial documentation to final approval.

Algeria's industrial safety regulations are primarily governed by Décret Exécutif 21-257, which establishes requirements for workplace safety, equipment standards, and employee protection measures. We ensure full compliance with these regulations.

Gas-In authorization requires a complete technical dossier including process flow diagrams, safety studies, equipment specifications, and conformity certificates. Our team prepares and submits all required documentation to the relevant authorities.
